The background

Built from running businesses, not from selling to them.

Before Corporate Saving Solutions, Andrew spent more than three decades owning and operating businesses across hospitality and leisure. That meant dealing first-hand with the same pressures CSS clients face today: utilities, suppliers, payments, technology, property, staff and cash flow.

Over time, that experience created a network of trusted providers and commercial relationships. CSS formalised that network into one point of contact for businesses that want practical, relationship-led support.

The philosophy

Know what you know.
Know who to call for the rest.

Andrew’s approach is deliberately straightforward: understand the commercial issue, bring in the right specialist where needed, and stay involved so the client is not left managing another disconnected supplier relationship.
